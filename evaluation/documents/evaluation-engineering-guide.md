# Northstar Engineering Operations Guide

## Production Changes - ENG-DEP-204

Routine production deployments must stop at 16:00 UTC every Friday. Emergency changes after the freeze require incident commander approval and a recorded change ticket.

## Incident Response - ENG-INC-310

The on-call engineer must acknowledge a critical production incident within 10 minutes. The incident channel and timeline must be opened immediately after acknowledgement.

## Code Review - ENG-REV-115

Pull requests should receive an initial review within one business day. Stale pull requests must be reviewed or closed after five business days.

## Dependency Security - ENG-SEC-225

Application dependency scans run weekly. Critical findings must be triaged within one business day and remediated or formally accepted within seven days.
