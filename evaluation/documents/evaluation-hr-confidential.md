# Northstar Confidential People Plan

## Retention Program - HR-CONF-510

The 2027 retention program provides a target bonus equal to 12 percent of base salary for designated critical roles. Eligibility lists are maintained by HR and approved by the Executive team.

## Compensation Review - HR-CONF-520

The annual salary review cycle begins in March. Managers receive confidential compensation bands only after HR calibration is complete.

## Workforce Planning - HR-CONF-530

The 2027 voluntary attrition planning target is below 9 percent. Business-unit plans are reviewed quarterly by HR leadership.
