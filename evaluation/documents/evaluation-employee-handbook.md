# Northstar Employee Handbook

## Annual Leave - HR-LEV-101

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted in the people portal before travel is booked.

## Parental Leave - HR-LEV-205

Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The benefit applies equally to birth, adoption, and surrogacy.

## Remote Work - HR-WRK-110

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Teams designate shared collaboration days for in-person work.

## Learning Benefit - HR-LRN-120

Each employee has an annual learning budget of USD 1,000 for approved courses, books, and professional certifications.

## Business Travel - TRV-210

The standard hotel reimbursement limit is USD 180 per night before taxes. Exceptions require written approval from a department vice president.
