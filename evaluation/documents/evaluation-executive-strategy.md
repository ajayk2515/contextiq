# Northstar 2027 Executive Strategy

## Strategic Priorities - EXE-STR-100

Northstar has three priorities for 2027: improve platform reliability, expand into Canada and Germany, and increase operating efficiency.

## Financial Objective - EXE-FIN-220

The company targets an 18 percent operating margin by the fourth quarter of 2027. Investment proposals must explain their effect on this objective.

## Market Expansion - EXE-MKT-310

Canada is the first expansion market, followed by Germany. The Canada launch readiness review is scheduled before the Germany localization program begins.
