# Northstar Finance Control Policy

## Expense Receipts - FIN-EXP-101

An itemized receipt is required for every business expense of USD 25 or more. Employees must submit an expense report within 15 calendar days of the transaction.

## Vendor Approval - FIN-VEN-230

New vendor commitments of USD 10,000 or more require approvals from both the requesting department head and Finance. Procurement must record both approvals before signing.

## Forecast Submission - FIN-FOR-150

Department forecasts are submitted monthly by the fifth business day. Finance publishes the consolidated forecast after variance review.

## Record Retention - FIN-REC-410

Invoices, expense records, and approval evidence must be retained for seven years from the end of the relevant fiscal year.
